§ 02

The math behind the price.

Why this works

What it'd cost otherwise.

A part-time receptionist working evenings and weekends — assuming you can find one who'll take the shift — runs $2,400–$3,200/month before benefits. An answering service averages $1.20–$2 per call answered. A missed call costs an SMB an estimated $80–$240 in lost lifetime value.

Practice tier breaks even at roughly 40 saved calls per month. Most clients save that in the first weekend.

What we don't charge for.

Setup. Onboarding. The "implementation manager" role you've seen invoiced at $185/hr by larger vendors. Per-minute call billing. Calendar integration "modules." API "credits." We charge a clean monthly number, in CAD, and tell you the next year's price up front.

If your call volume jumps past your tier, we tell you and offer a clean upgrade — never a surprise overage bill.
