§ 04 — Process 14 days, four stages

From first call to fully live 
in two weeks.

No "implementation services" charged hourly. No 90-day onboarding. Most clients are taking real calls inside 14 days — and the agent gets sharper every week after that.

Day 01 — Listen Day 04 — Design Day 08 — Train Day 12 — Launch Day 15+ — Tune forever
§ 01

The full walkthrough.

Four stages, fourteen days
01
Listen
Days 01—03

We shadow your phone for a week.

A discovery call to understand your business, then we set up a forwarding line so we can hear (with permission) the kinds of calls your team handles. We pull patterns: peak hours, common questions, the three or four edge cases that keep coming up. By day three we know what your agent needs to be able to do — and what it absolutely shouldn't try.

60-min discovery Call shadowing Pattern map Scope doc
02
Design
Days 04—07

We script the scenarios, not the words.

We don't write a wooden script. We design conversational flows: what happens when a caller asks for hours, when they ask to book, when they're upset, when they want to speak to a person, when they call for the third time. We pick a voice (we'll send you 4-6 options to listen to), confirm tone, and write the rules for what the agent will and won't say.

Branching flows Voice selection Escalation rules Approval gate
03
Train
Days 08—11

We tune the voice on your real calls.

We build the agent against your actual call patterns and put it through hundreds of synthetic test calls — including the difficult ones (mumbled, accented, angry, kids in the background, noisy car). You hear the recordings. You flag what feels off. We tune. Repeat until you can't tell.

Voice tuning Edge-case testing Integration setup Daily review calls
04
Launch
Days 12—14

We go live quietly, then loud.

Soft launch first — the agent picks up only after-hours, or only overflow. You hear the first 100 real calls before we route 100% of traffic. Then we hand you the dashboard, train your team to read it, and schedule the weekly tune-up. From here on, your agent gets sharper every Friday.

Soft launch Dashboard handoff Team walkthrough Weekly tune-up

§ 03

The stack, since you'll ask.

Under the hood

The boring truth.

We're not building foundation models. We're using the best ones — currently a mix of OpenAI and Anthropic for reasoning, ElevenLabs and Cartesia for voice synthesis, Deepgram for transcription, and Twilio for telephony — and orchestrating them around your specific business.

The interesting work isn't picking the model. It's the prompt engineering, the conversational design, the failure-mode handling, the integration plumbing, and the tuning loop. That's where 90% of voice AI deployments fall apart, and that's where we spend our hours.

The portable part.

We're model-agnostic on purpose. As better models ship, we swap them in. As your needs grow, we layer in more capability. You don't have to repurchase your agent every 18 months.

Your data — call transcripts, customer info, business logic — stays in your control. Stored in your jurisdiction (Canadian region by default for Canadian clients), exportable any time, and never used to train anyone else's model.

Two weeks. Then you stop missing 62% of your calls. Then we tune it forever.
