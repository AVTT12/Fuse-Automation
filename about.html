§ A

Why we exist.

The note we wrote first
Manifesto
— v.01

Fuse started with a number. 62%. The percentage of calls to small businesses, after hours, that go to voicemail and never come back. For a clinic, that's a patient. For a contractor, that's a quote. For a salon, that's a Saturday.

We watched our friends — chiropractors, plumbers, accountants, two restaurant owners on Corydon — lose business not to competitors, but to silence. The phone rang. Nobody picked up. The customer called somewhere else.

The fix being sold to them was a chatbot on a website. But people don't open a website at 9pm with a sore back. They pick up the phone.

So we built voice agents that pick up. Real ones. The kind your grandmother wouldn't notice was AI until she was already booked in.

§ B

Four rules we won't compromise on.

Operating principles
i.

It must sound human.

If a caller has to ask "is this a robot?", we've already lost. Voice, cadence, and silence matter as much as the words.

Voice quality
ii.

It must know its limits.

Every agent we deploy has a "transfer to human" reflex hardwired in. AI handles the 80%. Humans handle what matters.

Escalation
iii.

It must earn trust fast.

We deploy quietly first, behind your existing line. We don't route 100% of traffic until you've heard 100 calls and slept on it.

Soft launch
iv.

It must belong to you.

Your scripts, your data, your callers. We don't train on your transcripts to sell anywhere else. Ever.

Privacy
v.

It must get better weekly.

Every Friday we review the week's calls, find the rough edges, and ship a tune-up. The agent on Monday is sharper than the one on Friday.

Continuous
vi.

It must be priced fairly.

SMB margins are tight. Our pricing reflects that. No setup fees that pretend to be "implementation services." No call minimums.

Pricing
— On the question of replacing people

We don't replace your receptionist. We replace the voicemail nobody listens to and the busy signal that costs you a customer.

The cost of not being in San Francisco.

We're closer to our customers — most of whom run businesses we recognize from our own neighbourhood. We charge less because we live in a city where rent doesn't eat 80% of revenue. We hire people who actually want to live here, and that means the team that builds your agent today is the team that tunes it next year.

The benefit of shipping from a winter city.

Winnipeg teaches you a few things. How to make do with what you've got. How to be patient. How to be funny about it. The kind of voice agents we build — direct, unflashy, useful — couldn't have come from anywhere else. We're proud of that.
