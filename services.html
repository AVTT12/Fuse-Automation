§ 01

What we actually ship.

Service catalogue
01
Reception

An inbound voice agent that picks up every call.

Your existing number, answered in your voice, 24/7. The agent greets the caller, qualifies what they need, books the appointment straight into your calendar, captures contact info, and transfers to a human if it sniffs anything urgent. Works during business hours as overflow, after hours as primary.

Calendar booking Lead qualification Live transfer SMS follow-up Call summaries to email
02
Outbound

An outbound assistant that calls back in under 60 seconds.

When a lead fills out your form or leaves a voicemail, the agent calls them back before they've closed the tab. Quotes and follow-ups stop slipping. Recall lists for clinics and salons get worked weekly without anyone lifting a phone. The agent logs every call, every outcome, into your CRM.

Lead callback Quote follow-up Recall lists Appointment reminders No-show recovery
03
Custom

A custom agent trained on your business, not a template.

For shops with sharp edges — multiple locations, complex booking rules, regulated industries, very specific brand voice. We sit with you, write the playbook together, build the agent against your actual call patterns, and integrate it with whatever stack you already run.

Custom playbook Multi-location routing Regulated workflows Stack-agnostic integration Brand voice matching
04
Bilingual

A bilingual deployment for businesses serving both languages.

Native EN and FR voices, with seamless mid-call language switching. Built for Manitoba, the Prairies, and any business where "bonjour" lands as warmly as "hi." Add Spanish, Tagalog, or Punjabi as a third channel — most of our clients do.

EN + FR native Mid-call switching Add'l languages on request Accent-stable

§ 03

Who it's built for.

Industries we know

Health & wellness practices.

Chiropractors, physiotherapists, dental clinics, vets, optometrists, mental health practices. The agent handles new-patient intake, reschedules, recalls, and triages "is this an emergency?" with proper escalation rules.

Trades & home services.

Plumbers, electricians, roofers, HVAC, locksmiths, cleaning services. The agent qualifies the job, gets the address, books the dispatch window, and texts the homeowner an arrival ETA.

Beauty & personal care.

Salons, barbershops, spas, med-spas, lash and brow studios. The agent handles bookings, reschedules, and recalls — without the front desk getting interrupted every 4 minutes.

Professional services.

Accountants, lawyers, mortgage brokers, real estate agents, financial advisors. The agent qualifies leads, schedules consults, and politely declines the spam calls.

Restaurants & hospitality.

Independent restaurants, cafés, food trucks, small hotels. The agent answers hours, takes reservations, handles large-party requests, and routes catering inquiries to the owner.

Anything local, really.
